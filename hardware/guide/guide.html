Selfie booth box

A laser-cut plywood box that holds the whole selfie booth: a 14 in MacBook Pro hidden in the base, an iPad mini behind a window in the front, and a Rollo label printer behind a print slot. Nine pieces of plywood, no screws, just wood glue. Finished size 393 x 270 x 265 mm (15.5 x 10.6 x 10.4 in).

Cut files

All nine pieces come laid out on seven 12 x 20 in sheets of 6 mm (1/4 in) plywood, ready to cut. Each sheet comes two ways: an SVG for the Glowforge app (red lines cut, black areas engrave) and a DXF for other lasers (CUT and ENGRAVE layers). Only the front has engraving.

Before cutting everything: measure your plywood. "1/4 in" plywood is often 5.2 to 5.8 mm, and the finger joints only fit when the files match it. Cut one corner joint first. If it's loose or tight, the files can be regenerated for your exact thickness (one number in hardware/src/box_dims.py).

Assembly

1

Glue the front, both sides and the bottom. The finger joints only go together one way. The slot near the top back of each side is for the lock bar.

2

Slide in the deck. Its four tabs go through the matching slots in the sides. The notch in its front edge goes on the left, behind the iPad window. Glue the tabs.

3

Glue on the top and let everything dry. The back stays off.

4

Put in the iPad. From underneath, push the USB-C cable up through the deck notch and plug it into the iPad. Stand the iPad on the deck, screen against the window, camera at the top. Press the holder plate's two tabs into the slots behind it. If the tabs are loose, a dot of glue holds them; tight, sand them a little.

5

MacBook, printer and labels. Plug the HDMI dummy plug and charger into the MacBook, close the lid, and slide it into the bay under the deck from the back. Set the printer on the deck, pushed right up to the front so its label lines up with the slot. Put the stack of 4 x 6 labels behind it and feed the first label into the printer's back slot. Run the printer cable and the iPad cable down through the deck to the MacBook.

6

Put on the back. Lead the power cords out through the notch at the bottom. Drop the back's two bottom tabs into the slots in the floor and tip it upright.

7

Slide in the lock bar. Facing the back of the box, push it in through the slot near the top of the left-hand wall, straight end first, through both walls behind the back panel, until the heart handle stops against the wall (as in the picture). To open the box later, pull the bar out by the heart.

8

Switch on. Plug in the power. The booth software, setup steps and the code to start are at amywork777.github.io/selfie-booth. Guests need a code to start (0000 to begin with; change it in Settings).

Refilling labels

Slide out the lock bar, tip the back out, drop a new stack behind the printer and feed the first label in.
