Selfie booth box

Laser cut files for the box. Type how thick your wood is and the files redraw to fit it.

1How thick is your wood?
mm

Measure it, ideally with calipers. "1/4 inch" wood is 6.35 mm on paper but is often a bit thinner.

2Download the files.
Every red line gets cut, holes included. Nothing is engraved.

In the Glowforge app, upload the sheets in the "Glowforge (SVG)" folder one at a time. Each sheet is 12 x 20 in. Other laser cutters: use the "Other lasers (DXF)" folder.

3See inside in 3D (optional): where the iPad, printer, print slot and paper go.

You can also drag to turn it and scroll or pinch to zoom.

4Put it together. Follow the assembly guide: glue the box, slide in the deck, then the iPad, printer and MacBook go in and the back drops on. No screws.
